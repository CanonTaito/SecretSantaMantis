## Fields

**Name**

Secret Santa Mantis

**Official website**

https://secretsanta.taitoapps.com/

**Short summary**

Free Secret Santa draw for families: households never draw each other, kids draw kids, and everyone gets their own secret link.

**Description**

Secret Santa Mantis is a free Secret Santa name draw made for families and mixed groups. Put people in households so partners and siblings never draw each other, mark children so kids only draw kids, and add your own "never draw" rules on top.

After the draw, each person gets their own private link. Opening it unwraps a present that shows who they're buying for, along with the budget, the date and that person's gift hint. Nobody sees the full list of matches, not even the organiser.

There's no sign-up and nothing to install. The list stays in the organiser's browser instead of an account: import names from a spreadsheet, tick off links as you send them, and download a backup file to move to another device.

**Features**

- Privacy focused
- Works on mobile
- Wishlist (gift hints)
- Ad-supported, because there's a sponsor banner and Amazon gift-idea links
